Zero-Panic Clinical Protocol

Why “Zero-Panic” Matters

Elderly individuals frequently skip or delay dosing by 15-40 minutes without clinical harm. Bombarding family members with emergency alerts triggers caregiver burnout and causes severe distress for patients.

Protocol Pillars

  • Grace Window: Default 45 minutes where reminders stay gentle and local to Eleanor's room.
  • Single Responsible Party: Only Sarah Vance receives Tier 1 nudges to avoid simultaneous chaotic calls.
  • No Double-Dose Directive: If a scheduled dosage passes the safe interval, the system locks the chamber and schedules clinical follow-up.
