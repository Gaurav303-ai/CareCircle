## Brand & Style

This design system embodies compassionate clinical authority balanced with tactile, welcoming warmth. Designed to bridge the needs of elderly adults, family members, and professional care teams, the aesthetic avoids sterile hospital tropes without lapsing into childlike oversimplification. 

The visual strategy merges **Modern Editorial Clarity** with **Soft Tactility**:
- **Trust & Reassurance:** Grounded, deep botanical greens signal institutional safety, longevity, and organic well-being.
- **Clarity Under Stress:** High-contrast typographic hierarchy and distinct state indicators give immediate peace of mind to anxious caregivers and visually impaired elders.
- **Dignity & Accessibility First:** Generous, tactile interactive areas (never below 44px) invite physical interaction without drawing stigmatizing attention to assistive requirements.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional baseline of calm capability. Surface foundations lean on luminous alabaster and warm off-white rather than harsh stark whites or clinical blues, reducing screen glare for aging eyes.

### Primary & Brand Core
- **Primary Deep Teal (`#13755D` / `#0F5A47` / `#0B4334`):** Used for primary calls-to-action, key headers, and core active navigation states. Communicates medical integrity and calm resolve.
- **Secondary Sage & Mint (`#29A383` / `#D1F0E4` / `#EBF8F3`):** Applied to supporting badges, soft card backgrounds, active list selections, and affirmative progress states.

### Intelligent Accent
- **AI Periwinkle (`#6366F1` / `#EEF2FF`):** Reserved exclusively for smart assistance, automated medication schedule insights, and predictive vitals monitoring to instantly separate machine-generated guidance from human medical notes.

### Health Status System
- **Adherence / Taken:** Fresh Forest Green (`#16A34A`, background `#DCFCE7`, border `#BBF7D0`).
- **Pending / Due Soon:** Warm Amber (`#D97706`, background `#FEF3C7`, border `#FDE68A`).
- **Missed / Critical Alert:** Soft Coral Red (`#DC2626`, background `#FEE2E2`, border `#FECACA`).

### Neutrals & Reading Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8F9FA` with secondary workspace canvas `#F3F5F4`.
- **Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` encased in precise structural borders (`#E2E8E5`).
- **Primary Type:** Deep Charcoal (`#1A2421` and `#2D3A35`), hitting WCAG AAA contrast ratios on pure white and tinted canvas surfaces.
- **Secondary Type:** Muted Slate (`#52665F`), reserved strictly for non-critical metadata and secondary timestamps.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, warm headlines with **Manrope** for body copy and data labels. 

- **Readability & Optical Comfort:** Body sizes skew slightly larger than standard enterprise software. The baseline reading size is `body-md` (16px / 1rem) with a relaxed `1.625` line height to accommodate varying reading distances and impaired vision.
- **Senior Mode Adaptability:** When high-legibility or senior accessibility modes are engaged, the UI shifts its base from `body-md` directly to `body-xl` (20px), while preserving numeric tabular alignments across vitals trackers and medication timers.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Use tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across dosages, blood pressure readouts, heart rates, and scheduling grids.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured **fluid grid** anchored by fixed maximum content widths (1440px for large desktop monitoring dashboards, 768px for single-stream patient logs, and full-width fluid layouts with safe bounds on mobile).

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Flow
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, stacked health timelines, full-width touch cards, and sticky bottom navigation containing primary actions (Medication Check-In, Emergency Dial, Care Chat).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` margins. Splits screen into patient overview rail and main schedule flow.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, `2.5rem` outer margins. Accommodates a persistent multi-carer sidebar, main health journal/vitals grid, and right-hand AI Care Assistant contextual panel.

### Touch Targets & Spatial Buffers
All actionable elements maintain strict minimum bounding boxes of `48px x 48px` (exceeding standard 44px parameters). Spacing between interactive rows must never fall below `space-sm` (8px) to prevent mis-taps by tremors or motor-impaired fingers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid of **tonal surface separation**, **crisp hairline containment**, and **soft ambient diffusion**. This eliminates visually noisy heavy shadows while ensuring components maintain definite boundaries against off-white backgrounds.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#F8F9FA`. Recessed areas, structural gutter backgrounds, and non-interactive dashboard containers.
- **Raised Surfaces (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` cards, timeline logs, and list rows. Bounded by a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8E5`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 90, 71, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Interactive Floating Panels (Level 2):** Flyouts, dropdown menus, and active calendar detail popovers: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 90, 71, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`, bordered by `#D1F0E4`.
- **Modals & Critical Overlays (Level 3):** Medication confirmation prompts and emergency dispatch drawers: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(11, 67, 52, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`, framed over an alabaster backdrop overlay with `rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.45)` tint and `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Roundedness Level 2** (`0.5rem` / `8px` default radius, scaling to `1rem` / `16px` on `rounded-lg` cards, and full pill rounding for status indicators).

- **Comforting Ergonomics:** Sharp right angles are strictly excluded to avoid institutional, clinical harshness. The moderate 8px-16px curvature retains clear alignment edges for multi-column dashboards while introducing an organic, humane softness.
- **Pill Geometry:** Pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) are exclusively reserved for quick-action touch triggers, health state chips, and notification avatars, reinforcing their tactile, pushable nature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Deep Teal (`#13755D`), white text, `height: 48px` minimum, `padding: 0 1.5rem`, `font-weight: 600`. Hover darkens to `#0F5A47`. Active state scales down gently (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** Surface pure white, 1.5px solid `#D1F0E4` border, `#13755D` label. Hover fills with `#EBF8F3`.
- **Urgent / Emergency:** Soft Coral Red (`#DC2626`), high-contrast white text, elevated slightly above neighboring UI.
- **Senior Accessibility Large Button:** Optional UI tier expanding height to `56px` with `body-xl` typography for single-action confirmations ("I Took My Medication").

### Chips & Status Badges
- Configured as pill shapes (`rounded-full`) with `padding: 0.25rem 0.875rem` and `label-sm` tracking.
- Composed with dual visual cues: always couple high-contrast colored text on muted pastel backdrops with an icon or dot indicator to satisfy color-blind accessibility (e.g., Green checkmark for *Taken*, Amber clock for *Due*, Red exclamation for *Missed*).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Minimum height `52px`, `1.5px` border in `#E2E8E5`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus states display a `2px` focus ring in Deep Teal (`#13755D`) with zero-pixel offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Scaled up to `24px x 24px` with an expanded invisible tap area of `48px`. When checked, fills with Deep Teal (`#13755D`) displaying a distinct 2.5px white check icon.

### Cards & Care Trackers
- Encased in `#FFFFFF`, with `rounded-lg` (16px) corners and a 1px `#E2E8E5` boundary. 
- Internal padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile. 
- Medication dose cards feature a designated left accent bar (4px width) reflecting scheduled urgency or status color.

### AI Care Assistant Panel
- Wrapped in a delicate `#EEF2FF` wash with an inner border of `#6366F1` at 20% opacity. 
- Accompanied by a distinct Periwinkle badge (`#6366F1`) and natural speech typography to differentiate automated caregiver summaries from human nurse directives.
